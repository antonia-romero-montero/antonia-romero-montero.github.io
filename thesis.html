Processing of Sexual Violence Complaints Against Adolescents in Chile: Evidence on Case Archiving, Media Salience, and Political-Territorial Context (2009–2019)

Master's thesis, Universidad Alberto Hurtado. Joint with Bárbara Boggiano and Carlos Díaz.

Abstract

Using administrative data from Chile's public prosecution service for 2009–2019, we study how sexual violence complaints involving adolescents aged 14 to 18 are handled. We show that most complaints are archived, and that the archiving rate is high and similar across prosecutors. We then ask whether media attention changes outcomes. Stacked event studies show that media salience increases the number of complaints filed but does not change archiving decisions. In a final, exploratory part, we examine how judicial behavior relates to legislative voting and social mobilization.

Methods

Stacked event studies (PPML), leave-one-out estimators, and wild cluster bootstrap.
