“It's Not Just Negligence”: The Economic Impact of Failing to Protect Children from Abuse and Violence

Policy paper written for the Economic Policy course, M.Sc. in Economics, Universidad Alberto Hurtado, June 2025.

Abstract

Every time an institution stays silent in the face of child abuse, it fails not only ethically: it also loses human capital, destroys future productivity, and deepens inequality.

In Chile, violence against children and adolescents continues to occur even in places that should protect them, and many institutions do not act. This omission is not just a moral failure; it has concrete and cumulative economic consequences. Although the Office of the Children's Ombudsperson (Defensoría de los Derechos de la Niñez) has existed since 2018, its capacity to intervene remains limited. This paper proposes strengthening it through binding legal powers, an autonomous budget, mandatory inter-ministerial coordination, a specialized unit on sexual violence in schools, and adolescent participation.

Protecting children is not only an ethical duty: it is a smart and urgent public investment.
